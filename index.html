The beginning

A meeting, then a rescue, then a nonprofit.

In 2006, UT alum Rick Reeder met Kenyan student John Kidenda through the Texas Exes job-shadowing program. When a family illness put John’s degree at risk, Rick and a small group of friends raised the fees so he could finish. In 2007 they formed African Leadership Bridge as a 501(c)(3).

The multiplier

Five thousand dollars unlocks a 7-to-1 return.

For every $5,000 ALB provides, UT offers about $34,000 in tuition benefits, dropping annual costs from about $50,000 to about $11,000.

Vision

A world where African leaders advance a more prosperous, interconnected future.

In partnership with the University of Texas at Austin, we shape exceptional African leaders through world class education, mentorship, and deep community engagement.

Pan-African network with global reach

We connect exceptional students from across Africa, strengthening peer learning, global collaboration, and long-term leadership networks that extend beyond borders.

Leveraged, sustainable investment

Our endowed scholarship model multiplies each dollar’s impact while providing students access to world-class education, networks, and long-term opportunity.

Embedded, high-touch support where students are

We are physically present alongside our students, providing access to consistent mentorship, community, networks and accountability that strengthens outcomes during their time at university and beyond.
